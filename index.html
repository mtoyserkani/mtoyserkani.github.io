About

I help organizations connect fragmented data and build the governance and operating foundations needed to use AI responsibly, especially in regulated and high-consequence environments. I lead product strategy, roadmaps, data governance, AI governance, and cross-functional operations that turn AI ambition into practical, trusted products.

Twenty years leading product strategy, technical program engineering, and operations across three regulated domains: pharma AI (Bayer, Moderna, Merck, Eli Lilly, FDA 21 CFR Part 11, GxP), enterprise data platforms (Genesys Cloud, GDPR, identity resolution), and aerospace safety-critical systems (F-22, F-35, C-17, Airbus A350, DO-178B). Full proof points are below.

Pharma & Life Sciences AI — Led product strategy and go-to-market for 50+ conversational AI and NLU-powered voice and chat products across global top-50 life sciences companies, including Bayer, Moderna, Merck, and Eli Lilly, as Head of cAI Products at conversationHEALTH. Drove over 70% of company revenue and contributed to its acquisition by Swoop in under four years. That customer 360 layer powers personalization across Genesys AI products: Predictive Routing, Virtual Agent, and Agent Copilot.
